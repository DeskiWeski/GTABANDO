SURFSIDE: WHO SNITCHED ON LUKEY?
A top-down, GTA-style crime game set in Eastlake, Ohio. One file. Free to host.

PUT IT ONLINE (GitHub Pages)
1. Go to your GitHub repo (or make a new one, e.g. "surfside").
2. Add file > Upload files > drag in index.html > Commit.
3. Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > main / root > Save.
4. After about a minute your link is https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPONAME/ . Text it to the group chat.
(index.html is the whole game. Nothing else is needed.)

THE GAME
Lukey got snitched on. You are the Regal Bandit Popstar, and you have until 6 AM to find the rat.
Search for evidence at the Surfside Convenience Market, then interview Corey Bapes, Boggy, Haunchi and BJ.
Each one makes you pass a challenge first:
  Corey   street race     Boggy  riff-off     Haunchi  shooting gallery     BJ  tail his car without being spotted
Every clue narrows the suspects (hat or no hat, big or lean, pickup / sedan / dirt bike). Track it in the case file (N).
When you are sure, go to Surfside Pier and accuse. Three wrong accusations ends the game.
Tom B is hunting you in his truck. Wreck it, wear him down on foot, then talk to him when he is winded.
Cops catch you or pull you over? Give up Lukey and they let you go. You only have three Lukey chips.
Watch for twists: Lukey's phone call from county (is he right?), the raid on the Regal, and the ending.
Your main weapon is the guitar-gun. It overheats, so ease off. Pierogi stands heal you.

CONTROLS (computer)
WASD / arrows   walk, or drive when in a car
Mouse           aim        Click or Space   fire guitar-gun        Q or right click   power chord
E               get in / out, talk, search           Shift   sprint (foot) / handbrake (car)
N               case file            Esc / P   pause            M   mute
Riff-off: D F J K (or arrow keys)

CONTROLS (phone)
Left thumb stick to move or steer. FIRE (drag it to aim), CHORD, USE, RUN/BRAKE, CASE buttons on the right. Turn the phone sideways.

HONEST NOTES
- Street names, landmarks and the order of things on Lake Shore Blvd are real Eastlake, OH
  (Lake Shore Blvd, Vine St, Erie Rd, Stevens Blvd, Bayshore Dr, Hiawatha Dr, Curtis Blvd, Surfside Pier, Eastlake Plaza, the Chagrin River, ...).
  The block-by-block geometry is simplified and approximated, not a map. Do not use it to navigate.
- Corey, Boggy, Haunchi and BJ are fictional cartoon versions of friends, using the names you gave.
  Everything they say and do is made up.
- Only the Bandit has a photo face (it is baked into the file). If you add photos of real people, get their OK
  before you post the game publicly.
